## UNIVERSAL PRE-START WALKROUND (ALL MACHINES)

### External Walkround (Before Entering Cab)

1. Ground conditions — contours, soft spots, mud, slopes
2. Survey pegs and markings — identify; do not disturb
3. Overhead services — power lines, height restrictions
4. Underground services — scan drawings, BYDA markings
5. Other plant and people — note all activity in working zone
6. Machine condition — walk completely around:
   - Fuel, oil, coolant levels (dipsticks/sight glasses)
   - Hydraulic fluid level and visible leaks under machine
   - Track/tyre condition — cuts, wear, inflation, missing pads
   - Undercarriage — debris, rock packing in tracks
   - All pins and locking devices — secure
   - Bucket/attachment — secure, no cracks, cutting edge wear
   - Lights, mirrors, cameras — clean and operational
   - ROPS/FOPS — no visible damage
   - Steps and handrails — secure, non-slip
   - Warning devices and decals — in place

### Pre-Start (In Cab — Before Starting Engine)

1. Seat adjustment — correct position
2. Seat belt — attached, retracts freely, webbing not damaged
3. Throttle — in appropriate start position (low idle/neutral)
4. Hydraulic controls — check for smooth operation; no leaks at base
5. Safety lockout lever — engaged (hydraulics locked out) before start
6. Dashboard gauges — all functioning; no warning lights

### After Engine Start / Warm-Up

1. Engine oil pressure — gauge shows pressure within seconds
2. Coolant temperature — allow to reach operating range before loading
3. Hydraulic controls — test all movements slowly at low idle
4. Re-check all gauges — fuel, temp, oil pressure normal
5. Report any faults — per company procedure before operating

**Attachments:** Standard bucket, rock/HD bucket, wide/cleanup bucket, hydraulic breaker, tilt rotator, vibratory plate compactor, quick-hitch coupler

### NZQA Unit Standard — US 17310 v3
**Operate a hydraulic excavator on infrastructure works sites | Level 3 | Credits 20**

| Outcome | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare — confirm job instructions, walkround, pre-start, attach bucket |
| 2 | Manoeuvre/control — clearances, slope control (max 30°), production, sequencing |
| 3 | Load and place — communication with truck driver, positioning, minimise spillage |
| 4 | Excavate, strip, trim — carry bucket 400–500mm above ground (200–300mm on slopes ≥15°) |
| 5 | Refuel, park, inspect — shutdown checklist, fault report |

**Evidence required:** Observed practical + supporting evidence from 3 separate excavation jobs

### VOC Requirements
- Machine ID, make/model/capacity/attachment
- Pre-start inspection demonstrated correctly
- Safe operating zone identification
- Swing/slew clearances demonstrated
- Loading onto trucks — communication and positioning
- Trenching to specified depth
- Safe shutdown and parking

### Manufacturer Training
- **CAT:** catoperatortraining.com — Level I Competent Operator, 40+ eLearning courses, simulator available
- **Komatsu:** komatsu.eu/en/driving-academy — simulator + machine-based; iMC GPS excavation training
- **Hitachi:** ThoroughTec simulators for ZX-series; Hitachi Technical Training Centre
- **Volvo:** Basic Operator Training — E-series playlist (YouTube); CareTrack operator coaching

### NZQA Unit Standard — US 17316 v3
**Operate a bulldozer on infrastructure works sites | Level 3 | Credits 20**

| Outcome | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare — walkround, pre-start (seat belt, throttle, hydraulics) |

- Blade type and ripper specified
- Tracks, undercarriage, blade pins, ripper pins, hydraulic lines inspected
- Demonstrated: pushing, windrowing, batter cutting, ripping, fine grading

## 4. MOTOR SCRAPER

### Overview & Types

Cuts, loads, hauls, and spreads material in one pass. Efficient for 100m–3km haul distances on bulk earthworks (subdivisions, highways, airports, dams).

- **Open bowl** — push-loaded by dedicated push dozer (CAT D8/D9)
- **Elevator** — self-loading; elevator chain mechanism
- **Push-pull** — two scrapers assist each other
- **Twin-engine** — separate engine on push axle; better on grades

### Manufacturers & Common Models

| Manufacturer | Common Models | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CAT | 613G, 615C, 621K, 623G, 627K, 631G, 637K | 631G/637K largest; push-pull on big projects |
| Volvo | S100C | Less common; EU market |

### NZQA Unit Standard — US 17314 v3
**Operate a motor scraper on infrastructure works sites | Level 3 | Credits 20**

| Outcome | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare — walkround, pre-start (visibility/seatbelt/engine/throttle/hydraulics), post-warm-up recheck |
| 2 | Manoeuvre/control — clearances; slope braking; smooth bowl/ejector; survey pegs; cycle optimisation |
| 3 | Transport materials — load and haul (cohesive, non-cohesive, wet AND dry); loading techniques; varying terrain/gradients; spread in layers; stockpiles; excavate/fill to line/level/gradient |
| 4 | Refuel, park, inspect |

**Loading techniques:** Push loading | Slope loading | Straddle loading | Pre-ripping | Elevator loading  
**Cycle optimisation factors:** Engine RPM, travel speeds, haul distances, cooperation with push dozer

### VOC Requirements

**Open Bowl:** Push loading demonstrated; correct cutting depth; ejector spreading; push-cat signals/distance  
**Elevator:** Self-loading demonstrated; elevator engaged at correct ground speed; bowl weight not exceeded; elevator off before road travel

## 5. ARTICULATED DUMP TRUCK (ADT)

### Manufacturers & Common Models

| Manufacturer | Common Models | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CAT | 725, 730, 735, 740 | On-board weighing; retarder system |
| Volvo | A25G, A30G, A35G, A40G | Market leader globally; CareTrack |
| Komatsu | HM300, HM400 | Strong in AU; growing NZ |
| Bell | B25E, B30E, B35E, B40E | Very common in NZ/AU |
| Doosan | DA30, DA40 | Growing presence |

**Key features:** Oscillation pivot joint, all-wheel drive, differential lock, retarder system, body raise inhibitor

### NZQA Unit Standard — US 17317 v3
**Operate an articulated dump truck on infrastructure works sites | Level 3 | Credits 20**

| Outcome | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare — walkround, hoist float/deck down, park brake on, clean deck between different materials |
| 2 | Receive loads — park in waiting area; service brake/neutral/park brake before loading; check rated load; move slowly after loading |
| 3 | Operate on haul road — retarder on downgrades; straddle wheel ruts; differential lock slippery conditions; cornering/braking/cross-slope |
| 4 | Dump materials — stockpile/fill area; uncontrolled tip head; reverse square to windrow |
| 5 | Shutdown/maintenance — engine run-down, hoist position, housekeeping, lubrication |

**Key rules:** Hoist in float/deck down before driving | Park brake before loading | Never exceed rated load

### VOC Requirements (VOC ARTICULATED DUMPTRUCK)
- Pivot joint inspection: pins, retaining bolts, grease
- Hoist cylinders: fluid levels, seals
- Tyre inspection: dual wheel bolt check, tyre pressures
- Demonstrated: waiting correctly for loading, receiving load, haul road driving, dumping (controlled and uncontrolled)

## 6. SELF-PROPELLED ROLLER (COMPACTION)

### Roller Types
- **Smooth drum vibratory** — subgrade, base course, asphalt (most common)
- **Padfoot/sheepsfoot** — cohesive soils (clay), subgrade
- **Pneumatic tyred** — chipseal and asphalt finish rolling
- **Combination roller** — front smooth drum + rear pneumatic (asphalt)
- **Tandem drum** — asphalt finish rolling

### Manufacturers & Common Models

| Manufacturer | Common Models | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CAT | CS11, CS533, CP573, CS76 | Compaction meter (CMV); GPS compaction mapping |
| Dynapac | CA2500, CA3500, CC1300 | Swedish; common NZ roading |
| Bomag | BW211, BW226, BW213 | Asphalt Manager intelligent compaction |
| Hamm | HD13, HD14, H13i | Wirtgen Group |
| Sakai | SV525, SW994 | Common NZ/AU markets |
| Volvo | SD115B, DD90B, PT220 | Used widely in NZ |

### NZQA Unit Standard — US 17315 v3
**Operate a self-propelled roller on infrastructure works sites | Level 3 | Credits 20**

| Outcome | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare — walkround, pivot points, hot/cold checks, post-warm-up recheck |
| 2 | Manoeuvre/control — clearances, slope operation, wet/slippery/rough ground, survey pegs |
| 3 | Compact materials — primary/secondary compaction with overlaps; vibration/static/knead; clay/silt/roading aggregates/roading materials; asphalt/chipseal OR aggregates |
| 4 | Refuel, park, inspect |

**Compaction methods:** Vibration (subgrade, base course, granular) | Static (near structures) | Knead/padfoot (cohesive soils)  
**Overlap:** Minimum 1/3 drum width overlap between passes  
**NZ specs:** NZTA M4 (basecourse), NZTA T/3 (field compaction), TNZ P/9 (asphaltic concrete)

### VOC Requirements (VOC PAVEMENT ROLLER)
- Roller type, vibration frequency and amplitude settings confirmed
- Material type confirmed (aggregate, asphalt, clay)
- Pass pattern and number of passes documented
- Achieved density confirmed (nuclear density gauge or DCP result)

## 9. ROAD SAW / CONCRETE CUTTING SAW

### Types
- Walk-behind push saw
- Self-propelled power-drive saw
- Ride-on saw (large cuts)
- Handheld saw (US 29028 — separate unit standard)

### NZQA Unit Standard — US 19675 v4
**Describe and operate a road saw | Level 3 | Credits 4**

**Blade Selection:**

| Surface | Blade Type |
|---|---|
| Asphalt | Diamond hard segments — faster, constant depth, less dust |
| Chipseal | Carbon OR diamond soft segments |
| Concrete | Diamond soft segments — wet cutting preferred |
| Steel-reinforced concrete | Carbon blade ONLY — diamond MUST NOT be used on rebar |

**Evidence:** 3 operations on ONE surface type (asphalt, chipseal, OR concrete)  
**Safety:** Dust/slurry management; kickback prevention; PPE (face shield, hearing, dust mask, gloves)
