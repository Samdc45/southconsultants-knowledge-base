## Competitive Research by Topic Cluster

### 1. Civil Construction Site Safety Induction

**Industry Standard Modules (Site Safe NZ, CHASNZ, Connexis):**
- Site hazard identification and risk assessment
- PPE requirements and fit
- Emergency procedures and muster points
- Health and Safety Act obligations (worker vs PCBU)
- Incident reporting and near-miss culture
- Fatigue management and fitness for work
- Drug and alcohol policy
- Environmental compliance (site run-off, waste)
- Working near plant and exclusion zones
- Permit to work systems
- Toolbox talk protocols

**SC Coverage:** Hazard recognition, PPE, health obligations, exclusion zones, permit to work.
**Missing:** Fatigue/fitness module, environmental compliance, drug & alcohol standalone, near-miss reporting flow.
**Gap Score: 7/11**

---

### 2. Excavator Operator Training

**Industry Standard (NZQA Unit Standards, Cat Operator Training, Conexis):**
- Pre-start inspection checklist (walkaround)
- Controls familiarisation and safe operating range
- Digging techniques — benching, battering, shoring
- Working near buried services — BYDA protocol
- Swing radius hazards and exclusion zones
- Load ratings and stability on slopes
- Safe shutdown and park-up procedures
- Environmental — tracking damage, fuel spill response
- Night/low-visibility operation
- Communication with spotters/STMS

**SC Coverage:** Excavation safety, trenching hazards, working near services, exclusion zones.
**Missing:** Pre-start inspection module, load ratings/slope stability, environmental damage/fuel spill, night operation, shutdown procedures.
**Gap Score: 5/10**

---

### 3. Underground Utility Strike Prevention

**Industry Standard (BYDA, Connexis, WorkSafe NZ):**
- BYDA process
- Service location methods — scanning, potholing, CAD overlay
- Exclusion zone establishment and monitoring
- Identifying service markers and asset maps
- Emergency response to a utility strike (gas, water, power, comms)
- Contractor vs asset owner responsibilities
- Documentation and permit requirements
- Case studies — recent strike incidents

**SC Coverage:** BYDA, exclusion zones, asset maps, permits.
**Missing:** Emergency response to a strike, case studies module, contractor/asset owner responsibilities, **Module 2 entirely absent from course 9**.
**Gap Score: 5/8 + 1 broken module (CRITICAL)**

---

### 4. Compaction & Earthworks

**Industry Standard (Connexis, Caterpillar, NZTA pavement specs):**
- Soil classification — cohesive vs granular
- Compaction theory — proctor density, moisture content
- Compaction equipment types — roller selection guide
- Testing methods — nuclear density gauge, sand cone, DCP
- Lift thickness limits per material type
- Subgrade preparation and proof rolling
- Common failures — bridging, rutting, moisture susceptibility
- NZTA M4, NZTA T/3 specification compliance
- Machine guidance integration (GPS grade control)

**SC Coverage:** Equipment operation, lift thickness, testing methods.
**Missing:** Soil classification deep-dive, proctor theory, failure modes module, NZTA spec compliance checklist.
**Gap Score: 5/9**

### 5. Safety Leadership in Civil Construction

**Industry Standard (AGC, ClickSafety, OSHAcademy — 8–12 modules):**
- Leadership vs management — what the crew sees
- Hazard observation and intervention skills
- Psychological safety — speaking up culture
- Pre-task planning and toolbox talk facilitation
- Incident investigation — 5 Whys, root cause analysis
- Leading indicators vs lagging indicators
- Worker engagement and recognition frameworks
- Fatigue, stress, and mental health awareness on site
- Safety culture maturity models
- Documentation — SWMS, JSA, site safety plans

**SC Coverage: 0/10 — Courses 21 & 26 are completely empty shells.**

### 6. Winning Civil Construction Tenders

**Industry Standard (EEA, Bid Perfect Academy, Udemy — 8–10 modules):**
- Reading and interpreting tender documents (RFP, IFT, ROI)
- Work breakdown structure (WBS) and scope definition
- Pricing strategy — direct costs, margins, contingencies
- Client T&Cs — key risk clauses to negotiate
- Compliance and prequalification requirements
- Writing a compelling methodology section
- Subcontractor procurement and pricing
- Submission formatting and evaluation criteria
- Post-tender debrief and continuous improvement
- NZ-specific: GETS portal, Constructing Excellence NZ

**SC Coverage: 0/10 — Course 22 is a completely empty shell.**

---

### 7. Traffic Management / CoPTTM → NZGTTM (NOT IN SC CATALOG — CRITICAL GAP)

**Industry Standard (NZTA, Parallaxx, TTM Training & Compliance, McAdams):**
- Code of Practice for Temporary Traffic Management — now transitioning to NZGTTM (from Nov 2024)
- Level 1 Traffic Controller certification
- STMS (Site Traffic Management Supervisor) Level 2
- Traffic Management Plan (TMP) preparation
- Cone and barrier setup patterns
- Signage and delineation requirements
- Night works and low-visibility protocols
- Pedestrian management through worksites
- Liaison with NZTA and road controlling authorities

**SC Coverage: ZERO — No TTM course exists.**
This is a core requirement for any NZ civil construction worker working near roads. Every contractor trains to this standard. Highest-priority new course to build.

---

### 8. Confined Space Entry (NOT IN SC CATALOG — GAP)

**Industry Standard (WorkSafe NZ, OSHA — 8 modules):**
- Definition and classification of confined spaces
- Permit-required vs non-permit confined spaces
- Atmospheric testing — oxygen, combustibles, toxics
- Ventilation requirements
- Entry supervisor, attendant, and entrant roles
- Personal protective equipment for CS entry
- Rescue planning and emergency procedures
- Lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) in confined spaces

**SC Coverage: ZERO.** Relevant for civil work in manholes, culverts, tanks, underground structures.

---

### 9. Working at Heights / Fall Prevention (NOT IN SC CATALOG — GAP)

**Industry Standard (WorkSafe NZ, Safety Culture — 10 modules):**
- Legislation and duty of care (Health and Safety at Work Act 2015)
- Hierarchy of fall protection controls
- Scaffolding types and inspection
- Ladders — selection, inspection, safe use
- Mobile Elevated Work Platforms (MEWPs/EWPs)
- Personal fall arrest systems — harness, lanyard, anchor
- Guardrail and safety net systems
- Roof and elevated slab work
- Rescue from height procedures
- Inspection and tag-out of fall protection equipment

**SC Coverage: ZERO.** Mentioned in induction only. No dedicated course.
